# Every Walk is recorded; the first passing one is the proof

The handoff rules say to walk until everything passes and then record each video in a separate clean pass (`shared-skill-core/handoff/rules.md`, "When every slice of a layer is green", step 4). With proofbox, recording costs nothing extra, but a second pass costs another walk of Mac minutes. So `/implement` records every Walk, discards a failed one with `record stop --discard`, and keeps the first Walk that passes as the proof. Human pace and the Still part cut keep that video easy to follow, even where the Walker paused to think.

## Considered options

- A separate recording pass after all Walks pass: a fully planned run on video, but one more walk of Mac time per video.
